# Code of conduct

**English** | [Русский](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

The project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) by reference, without copying the text. The full text is at the link; if a translation differs from the original, the original governs. A short summary follows.

## In short

- **Expected:** respect for people and their experience, constructive criticism, owning your mistakes, attention to the interests of the community as a whole.
- **Unacceptable:** insults and demeaning comments, trolling, harassment, publishing others' private information without consent, sexualized language and attention, any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.
- **Where it applies:** in all project spaces (issues, PRs, Discussions, reviews) and when a person represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove comments, issues, PRs and other contributions that do not follow the code, and may restrict participation. Measures follow the Contributor Covenant ladder: correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban.

Report a violation to the maintainer privately, using the contacts in the profile [@Windemiatrix](https://github.com/Windemiatrix). Reports are handled confidentially; the reporter must not fear retaliation. Do not report vulnerabilities here: follow [SECURITY.en.md](SECURITY.en.md).
